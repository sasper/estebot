{# 3. How it works #}

{{ icon("steps") }}How it works

  1. 1

    {{ icon("connect") }}Connect what you already use

    Apple Health for sleep, HRV, resting heart rate and weight. Hevy for lifting. Cronometer for food. Pulled in automatically.

  2. 2

    {{ icon("checkin") }}Check in, get one call

    A couple of taps after you wake up. Then one call: go hard, go as planned, take it easy, or rest, with the reasons written out.

  3. 3

    {{ icon("session") }}Open Hevy and lift

    Your coach has already picked today's training. Your Hevy routine is updated from your own signals, with a target for every exercise.

{# 4. Why it's different #}

{{ icon("spark") }}Why it's different

{{ icon("math") }}Numbers from math, not guesses

Every figure comes from tested algorithms. The AI explains them. It never makes one up.

{{ icon("grade") }}It grades itself

Every call is checked against how the session went. A signal only gets a say once your own history shows it matters for you.

{{ icon("fix") }}Messy data, fixed at the source

Unit switches, renamed exercises, double-counted sleep: fixed by rules, so years of history rebuild the same way every time.

{{ icon("ask") }}Ask it anything about your data

Plain-English answers from your own numbers.
