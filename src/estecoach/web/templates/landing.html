{# 3. How it works #}

How it works

  1. Connect what you already use. Apple Health for sleep, HRV, resting heart rate and weight. Hevy for lifting. Cronometer for food. Pulled in automatically.
  2. Check in, get one call. Thirty seconds of taps after you wake up. Then one call: go hard, go as planned, take it easy, or rest, with the reasons written out.
  3. Open Hevy and lift. Today's session is already in your routine, with a target for every exercise.
{# 4. Why it's different #}

Why it's different

Numbers from math, not guesses. Every figure comes from tested algorithms. The AI explains them. It never makes one up.
It grades itself. Every call is checked against how the session went. A signal only gets a say once your own history shows it matters for you.
Messy data, fixed at the source. Unit switches, renamed exercises, double-counted sleep: fixed by rules, so years of history rebuild the same way every time.
Ask it anything about your data. Plain-English answers from your own numbers.
